# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include respect, constructive communication, and cooperation.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and interactions related to the project.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reviewed by the project maintainers and appropriate action may be taken.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will consider the circumstances and impact of each violation when deciding how to respond.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from common open-source community standards.
